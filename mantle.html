Features

Procure to Pay

  • Manage suppliers
  • Purchase orders for inventory, equipment, supplies
  • Receiving and warehouse management
  • Accounts payable
  • Learn from Procure to Pay tests

Order to Cash

  • Support and manage customers
  • Sales orders for goods, subscriptions, services
  • Shipping and tax calculation (Drools rules or integration)
  • Inventory management and fulfillment
  • Accounts receivable and payment processing
  • Learn from Order to Cash tests

Return to Response

  • Customer and supplier returns
  • Customer credit, refund, replacement
  • Authorize and respond auto or manual
  • Learn from Return to Response tests

Work Plan to Cash

  • Client requests
  • Projects, milestones, and tasks
  • Flexible rates, time and expenses
  • Bill clients, pay employees and subcontractors
  • Learn from Work Plan to Cash tests

Product Information

  • Goods, services, subscriptions, equipment types
  • Classes, categories, features, faceted keyword search
  • Flexible pricing (and modify with Drools rules)
  • Content, identification, dimensions, associations, reviews

Asset Management

  • Inventory, equipment, supplies, and more
  • GL posting for issuance, receipt, depreciation, physical inventory
  • Inventory projections from reservations, shipments, production runs
  • Equipment scheduling and maintenance

HR and Payroll

  • Positions, classes, employees
  • Flexible hourly and piece rates, salaries
  • Payroll tax calculation (federal, state, local; employee and employer shares), garnishment
  • Pay wages, taxes, garnishments by check, ACH, and more

Accounting

  • Flexible chart of accounts; reporting and posting based on type and class
  • Automated GL posting for invoices, payments, balance accounts, assets, production
  • Payment and GL reconciliation (OFX or other), NACHA payments, positive pay
  • Balance sheet, income, cash flow, equity; financial ratios and other reports

General

  • Multiple organizations, localization, and currencies
  • System-wide search and analytics
  • Access and audit data, configurable authorization and velocity limits
  • Comprehensive REST API
