# Story of Online Retail Company

Someone decides to sell a product. [Product Marketer Maintains Product Information](Product Marketer Maintains Product Information). 
[Buyer Places Purchase Order](Buyer Places Purchase Order). [Marketer Maintains General Site Content](Marketer Maintains General Site Content).

When new inventory is needed [Buyer Places Purchase Order](Buyer Places Purchase Order). 
When shipments arrive [Shipment Receiver Receives Shipment](Shipment Receiver Receives Shipment), then [Inventory Mover Puts Away Inventory](Inventory Mover Puts Away Inventory).

[Customer Finds Product](Customer Finds Product). [Placing Customer Adds Product to Cart](Placing Customer Adds Product to Cart) 
and [Placing Customer Places a Sales Order - Online Checkout](Placing Customer Places a Sales Order - Online Checkout). 
Alternatively [Placing Customer Places a Sales Order - Through CSR](Placing Customer Places a Sales Order - Through CSR) 
or [Placing Customer Places a Sales Order - Through eBay](Placing Customer Places a Sales Order - Through EBay).
If Customer decides to, [Customer Cancels Order](Customer Cancels Order).

If desired during checkout or for other reasons, [Customer Creates and Edits Account](Customer Creates and Edits Account). 
As desired [Customer Reviews Order History and Status](Customer Reviews Order History and Status).

Once order is ready [Company Approves Sales Order](Company Approves Sales Order).

When inventory is not sufficient in a pick location [Inventory Mover does Stock Move](Inventory Mover does Stock Move). 
[Company Fulfills Sales Order](Company Fulfills Sales Order). [Accountant Sends Invoice and Receives Payment](Accountant Sends Invoice and Receives Payment). 
Sometimes special cases come up, like [Carrier Does Not Deliver Shipment to Customer](Carrier Does Not Deliver Shipment to Customer) 
and [Carrier Returns Undeliverable Shipment to Company](Carrier Returns Undeliverable Shipment to Company).

[Customer Returns Product](Customer Returns Product). [Company Processes Return Received](Company Processes Return Received).
