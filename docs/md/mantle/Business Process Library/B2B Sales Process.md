B2B Sales High Level Business Process Story

MARKETING:(MARKET TO WARM LEAD/REQUEST)
* Demand Generation, Qualification, and Routing (move to dedicated Marketing business processes page)
  * Marketer executes marketing campaign
  * Customer submits contact form
  * Customer subscribes to notifications
  * Marketer records and tracks lead
  * Marketer evaluates lead
  * Marketer routes lead to call center
  * Marketer routes lead to sales person
  * Marketer nurtures leads (drip campaigns)

SALES (LEAD EVALUATION/ROUTING TO ORDER PLACEMENT/APPROVAL)
* Sales Lead Management
  * Sales person reviews assigned leads
  * Sales person accepts lead
  * Sales person rejects lead
  * Sales person contacts lead
* Account and Opportunity Management
  * Sales person converts lead to sales account and contact
  * Sales person records an opportunity
  * Sales person adds opportunity to sales pipeline
  * Sales person schedules meeting with customer
  * Sales person prepares presentation
  * Sales person meets with customer
  * Sales person moves opportunity to next stage
  * Sales person prepares proposal and sends to customer
  * Sales person moves opportunity to next stage
  * Customer accepts proposal and requests formal quote
  * Sales person creates quote
  * Sales person submits quote for approval
  * Sales manager approves quote
  * Deal desk approves quote
  * Sales person requests contract
  * Legal creates contract
  * Legal sends contract to customer for review and signature
  * Customer signs contract
  * Customer returns contract
  * Sales rep is notified that contract has been signed
* Forecast and Pipeline Management
  * Sales manager reviews sales forecast
  * Sales manager reviews deals with sales reps
